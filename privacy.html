Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how Forty Sided Studio ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with our games and apps, including Streben Der Jäger (the "App").

Who we are

Forty Sided Studio is an independent, single-developer games studio. You can reach us at fortysidedstudio@gmail.com.

Information we collect

The App is designed to work primarily offline and does not require an account to play. We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or contact list.

If the App uses any third-party services in the future (for example, crash reporting, analytics, or advertising), those services may collect limited technical data (such as device model, operating system version, or app crash logs) as necessary to operate. This document will be updated to reflect any such service before it is enabled, and the Data Safety section of the App's Google Play listing will always reflect the current, accurate state.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Where any third-party service is used (see above), data is only shared with that service to the extent needed for it to function, subject to that service's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Since the App does not require registration and stores gameplay data locally on your device by default, you can remove all local App data at any time by uninstalling the App.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App evolves. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to fortysidedstudio@gmail.com.
