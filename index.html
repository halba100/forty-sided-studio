The studio

Forty Sided Studio is a one-person forge run by someone who has been at the table since he was eight — decades of tabletop campaigns, home computers, and grid-paper dungeons distilled into games that don't apologise for being difficult, dark, or a little obsessive about their own lore.

No publisher, no committee. Just a dev, a stack of notebooks, and a long-standing appetite for hex grids and grim skies.

Current project

Streben Der Jäger

A hex-based tactical RPG, set after the fall of the Holy Roman Empire.

The Cataclysm did not end the war — it just changed who's still fighting it. Move phase by phase across broken hex ground, choose when to strike and when to hold the line, and drag what's left of an empire through six turns of steel, magic, and monster tides.
